# FreeDomain

**`DigitalPlatDev/FreeDomain`** · ⭐ 183,409 · 🔧 HTML

## What is it?
FreeDomain is a free domain registration platform offering multiple TLDs including .DPDNS.ORG, .US.KG, .QZZ.IO, .XX.KG, and .QD.JE. The project enables users to register unique domain names without cost and integrate them with popular DNS providers like Cloudflare, FreeDNS by Afraid.org, or Hostry. It is independently designed and maintained by DigitalPlat Foundation under Edward Hsing.

The platform provides a dashboard for domain management and registration, with documentation available for tutorial guidance. Over 500,000 domains have already been registered through this service, demonstrating significant adoption in the free domain space.

## How does it work?
The platform operates as a web-based domain registration service with a public-facing dashboard accessible via dash.domain.digitalplat.org. Users can browse available TLDs, register domains through the interface, and then configure DNS records using their preferred provider's control panel (Cloudflare, FreeDNS, or Hostry). The system appears to use a backend infrastructure for storing domain records and managing registrations.

The open-source nature of the project allows community contributions and transparency in how domains are provisioned and managed.

## Difference from Others
Unlike commercial domain registrars like Namecheap, GoDaddy, or Google Domains that charge annual fees, FreeDomain costs nothing and is maintained as open-source by DigitalPlat Foundation. This makes it particularly valuable for self-hosting workflows where budget-conscious infrastructure setup is needed. It also provides a wider variety of TLDs than typical registrars, giving you more options for domain naming without paying premium prices.
