# OpenVoice

> **TL;DR:** Zero-shot text-to-speech with accurate tone color cloning and multi-lingual support.

**`myshell-ai/OpenVoice`** · ⭐ 36,870 · 🔧 Python

## What is it?
OpenVoice is an audio foundation model for instant voice cloning, developed by MIT and MyShell AI. It enables zero-shot cross-lingual text-to-speech without requiring prior training on the target speaker or languages. The project addresses two core challenges in synthetic speech: accurately replicating a reference speaker's tone color while generating natural-sounding speech in multiple languages and accents, and providing flexible control over voice styles including emotion, rhythm, pauses, and intonation.

## How does it work?
OpenVoice operates as an audio foundation model that extracts speaker features from a short reference sample and applies them to text-to-speech generation. The architecture separates speaker representation from linguistic content, allowing zero-shot cross-lingual cloning where neither the generated language nor the reference language needs to appear in training datasets. OpenVoice V2 introduced an improved training strategy that delivers better audio quality while retaining all V1 capabilities.

## Why is it important? (Core Value)
For your objectives, OpenVoice offers significant value as a self-hostable alternative to cloud-based TTS services like Azure Speech or Google Text-to-Speech. The zero-shot cloning capability means you can generate voices without needing extensive speaker data, making it ideal for building AI agents with personalized voice identities. Its flexible style control (emotion, accent, rhythm) aligns well with your interest in AI/LLM tooling and could integrate into agent workflows that require dynamic speech generation. Additionally, as an MIT-backed open-source project from MyShell AI, it represents credible research infrastructure you could incorporate into homelab deployments or automation pipelines.

## Key Features & Technologies
- Zero-shot text-to-speech cloning
- Accurate tone color replication
- Flexible style control (emotion, accent, rhythm)
- Cross-lingual voice generation
- Native multi-lingual support (English, Spanish, French, Chinese, Japanese)
- Open-source audio foundation model

## Difference from Others
Compared to other TTS models like VALL-E or Coqui TTS, OpenVoice stands out for its zero-shot capability—cloning voices without prior training data—and its granular style controls that separate emotional and accent parameters from linguistic content. Unlike commercial services requiring speaker enrollment, it works with minimal reference audio. This makes it particularly suitable for agent applications where voice identity must be generated on-the-fly rather than pre-trained.
