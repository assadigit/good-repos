## What is it?
The sports repository is a collection of reusable computer vision tools specifically designed to tackle challenging sports analytics problems. Roboflow built this as a testing ground to push their object detection, image segmentation, keypoint detection, and foundational models to their limits. The repository focuses on real-world difficulties like tracking small, rapidly moving balls in high-resolution videos, reading jersey numbers through occlusions and blurry footage, maintaining consistent player identification during games, and re-identifying players who leave and re-enter the frame.
