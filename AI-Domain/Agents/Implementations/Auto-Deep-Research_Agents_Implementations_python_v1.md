# Auto-Deep-Research

> **TL;DR:** Fully-automated AI agent that performs deep research by searching, reading, and synthesizing information from multiple sources.

**`HKUDS/Auto-Deep-Research`** · ⭐ 1,721 · 🔧 Python

## What is it?
Auto-Deep-Research is a fully-automated, cost-effective personal AI assistant designed for deep research tasks. It autonomously searches the web, reads and processes documents, and synthesizes findings into comprehensive research reports without requiring constant human intervention. The project is backed by a peer-reviewed arxiv paper (2502.05957) and is developed by the HKU Data Science group (HKUDS).

The system is designed to be a general-purpose research agent that can tackle complex, multi-step research questions. It emphasizes cost-effectiveness, making it accessible compared to proprietary AI research tools that rely on expensive frontier models. The project has an active community with Slack and Discord channels, along with dedicated documentation.

As an open-source project, it provides transparency into how deep research agents can be built and deployed, with the full methodology documented in its accompanying research paper.

## How does it work?
Auto-Deep-Research operates as an autonomous agent loop: it decomposes a research question into sub-tasks, performs web searches, reads and processes retrieved documents, iteratively refines its understanding, and synthesizes findings into a structured report. The architecture leverages LLMs for reasoning and synthesis while emphasizing cost-effectiveness, likely using a mix of smaller models for intermediate steps and larger models for final synthesis.

The project is grounded in academic research (arxiv 2502.05957), suggesting a structured methodology for the research pipeline. The agent autonomously manages the entire research workflow from query decomposition to final report generation, minimizing human intervention and API costs.

## Why is it important? (Core Value)
For a software engineer focused on AI agents and self-hostable alternatives, Auto-Deep-Research offers a production-quality, open-source deep research agent that can be integrated into development workflows. It directly addresses the need for self-hostable alternatives to SaaS research tools like Perplexity's AI Researcher or OpenAI's Deep Research, providing a transparent, cost-effective solution. The arxiv paper backing adds credibility for evaluating its approach, and the agent architecture could serve as a reference implementation for building custom research agents or MCP-compatible research skills. The cost-effectiveness angle is particularly relevant for developers running multiple agent pipelines who need to manage inference costs.
