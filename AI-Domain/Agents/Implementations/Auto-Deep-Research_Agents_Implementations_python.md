# Auto-Deep-Research

> **TL;DR:** Automated AI agent that performs deep research by searching, synthesizing, and generating comprehensive reports.

**`HKUDS/Auto-Deep-Research`** · ⭐ 1,721 · 🔧 Python

## What is it?
Auto-Deep-Research is a fully-automated AI agent system designed to serve as a personal AI assistant specializing in deep research tasks. Developed by HKUDS (HKU Data Science), it takes research questions and autonomously decomposes them into sub-tasks, searches for relevant information, and synthesizes findings into comprehensive, structured reports. The project is backed by an academic paper (arXiv:2502.05957) and emphasizes cost-effective operation, making it practical for real-world research workflows.

The system is positioned as an accessible alternative to expensive LLM-based research pipelines. It combines agent-based orchestration with LLM capabilities to automate the entire research process from question to final report, targeting users who need thorough research without manual effort.

The project maintains active community channels (Slack, Discord, Feishu, WeChat) and provides documentation through a dedicated project site, indicating an intent to serve as a practical tool rather than a purely academic artifact.

## How does it work?
The project uses an LLM-powered agent architecture to automate deep research. It employs a multi-step pipeline where an LLM agent decomposes research questions into sub-tasks, performs information retrieval (web search or document lookup), synthesizes findings, and generates structured reports. The 'cost-effective' design suggests strategic LLM usage—potentially routing intermediate steps through smaller/cheaper models and reserving larger models for final synthesis.

The agent operates autonomously, planning its research steps, executing them iteratively, and refining its output based on intermediate findings. This multi-agent or multi-step orchestration pattern is what distinguishes it from single-turn LLM queries, enabling comprehensive research that would be impractical to do manually.
