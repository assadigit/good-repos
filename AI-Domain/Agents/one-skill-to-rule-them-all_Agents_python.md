# one-skill-to-rule-them-all

> **TL;DR:** AI meta-skill that watches your work sessions and auto-generates/improves skills from observations.

**`rebelytics/one-skill-to-rule-them-all`** · ⭐ 898 · 🔧 N/A

## What is it?
Task Observer is an AI meta-skill implementing the Augmented Expertise methodology that automatically creates new skills by detecting repeating patterns in your work and drafts skill candidates. It also continuously improves existing skills by noticing your corrections, preferences, and gaps. In the first three months of use, it logged and applied over 600 improvements across 40 skills, most of which were themselves created based on observations by the meta-skill. The project is open-source, domain-agnostic, and released under CC BY 4.0.

## How does it work?
Task Observer operates as a background AI agent that monitors your digital work sessions. It uses LLM-based observation to detect repeating patterns in your actions and automatically drafts new skills from these observations. When you make corrections or express preferences during work, the agent captures these judgments and suggests specific updates. The system is designed to be autonomous and self-improving, continuously refining its own capabilities as it observes more of your workflow.

Unlike static skill libraries or traditional productivity tools, Task Observer is self-improving - it continuously creates and refines skills based on your actual work patterns. While other tools like Notion templates or Obsidian plugins provide structured knowledge, this project specifically targets the meta-skill concept where the system builds its own capabilities. Its integration with autonomous agent frameworks (Hermes, Openclaw) also differentiates it from general LLM tooling.
