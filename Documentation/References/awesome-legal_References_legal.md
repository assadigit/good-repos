# awesome-legal

> **TL;DR:** Curated collection of free legal documents and policies for companies and developers.

**`ankane/awesome-legal`** · ⭐ 1,093 · 🔧 N/A

## What is it?
awesome-legal is a curated reference repository that aggregates free legal documents, policies, and templates useful for companies and software developers. It covers a wide range of categories including site policies (terms of service, privacy policies), customer agreements, employee agreements, consulting agreements, advisor agreements, investor agreements, founder agreements, non-disclosure agreements, and libraries. The project is organized with clear sections and includes helpful indicators—documents written in plain language are marked with :tada: to make them easy to identify.

## How does it work?
The repository is a simple markdown-based curated list. Each entry typically contains a title, link to the original document, and optional notes or tags. There is no complex architecture or runtime involved—it serves as a static reference resource that developers can browse, search through its table of contents, and click links to access the actual legal documents on their respective websites or repositories.

## Why is it important? (Core Value)
This project directly supports your objectives by providing a centralized collection of legal documents you can reference when building self-hosted platforms or open-source projects. It includes policies from major tech companies like GitHub (its own site policy), Automattic (legalmattic), and Y Combinator—aligning with your interest in open-source projects from major companies. As a researcher and developer, having quick access to plain-language legal templates saves time and ensures you're working with well-documented agreements rather than reinventing basic terms of service or privacy policies.

## Key Features & Technologies
- Curated list of legal documents
- Plain language indicators (:tada:)
- CC0 1.0 public domain license
- Covers major tech company policies
- Organized by category

## Difference from Others
Unlike generic legal document repositories that may require registration or have licensing restrictions, this project focuses on free, openly licensed documents with clear plain-language markers for easy identification. It also aggregates policies from major tech companies in one place, which distinguishes it from scattered legal resources across individual company websites.
