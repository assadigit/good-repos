# Marketing-for-Founders

> **TL;DR:** Curated list of practical marketing resources to help SaaS founders acquire early users without budget.

**`EdoStra/Marketing-for-Founders`** · ⭐ 6,697 · 🔧 N/A

## What is it?
Marketing-for-Founders is a curated markdown repository containing practical marketing resources specifically tailored for early-stage SaaS founders and technical creators with limited budgets. It focuses on actionable, real-world advice rather than inspirational content about scaling VC-funded companies to millions in ARR.

The project is organized into topic-based sections covering the full marketing funnel from initial user acquisition through growth. Topics include Product Hunt launch strategies, SEO fundamentals and emerging LLM/voice search optimization, social media marketing across platforms, sales and cold outreach techniques, email marketing best practices, content marketing approaches, paid advertising guidance, influencer marketing, affiliate/referral programs, free-tool marketing tactics, and landing page/messaging frameworks.

This repository serves as a reference collection that founders can consult when building their Go-To-Market strategy. Each section provides curated links to tools, guides, templates, and communities that have proven useful for getting the first 10/100/1,000 users without requiring substantial marketing budgets.
