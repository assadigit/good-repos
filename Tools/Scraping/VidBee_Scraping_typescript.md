# VidBee

> **TL;DR:** Download videos from almost any website worldwide.

**`nexmoe/VidBee`** · ⭐ 9,901 · 🔧 TypeScript

## What is it?
VidBee is a cross-platform video downloader application that supports downloading videos from a wide variety of websites including YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Twitter/X, Instagram, Twitch, and many more. The tool provides a user-friendly interface for pasting URLs and initiating downloads, making it accessible to both casual users and power users who want to archive or convert video content.

## How does it work?
The application likely works by parsing website HTML/JSON endpoints to detect media stream URLs (such as HLS or DASH streams), then downloading those streams directly to disk. It probably includes logic for handling proxy settings, output path configuration, and may offer options for quality/format selection during download.

Some alternatives may cover fewer platforms or require browser extensions; VidBee appears to consolidate support across many major video sites in a single application.
