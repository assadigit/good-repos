# flowsint

**`reconurge/flowsint`** · ⭐ 7,432 · 🔧 TypeScript

## What is it?
Open-source OSINT graph exploration platform for ethical investigation, transparency, and verification. Built with Docker and Make.

## How does it work?
Runs in Docker containers orchestrated by Make. Ingests OSINT data from various sources (as implied by its focus on investigation, OSINT, recon) and constructs a graph of entities and relationships. Early development stage with community contributions.

## Why is it important? (Core Value)
Provides a self-hosted alternative to proprietary OSINT SaaS tools, aligning with your interest in self-hostable software and automation. The Apache-2.0 license ensures free use and modification. ETHICS.md gives responsible usage guidelines, supporting your ethical software focus. While not an AI agent or MCP server, it can serve as a data source for investigations that feed into AI-driven analysis or be integrated into your Obsidian vault for documentation.

## Key Features & Technologies
- Docker

## Difference from Others
Unlike commercial graph tools like Maltego or automation-focused OSINT platforms like Recon-ng and SpiderFoot, Flowsint emphasizes ethical guidelines, self-hosting, and community-driven development with funding channels. It targets investigation workflows rather than just data collection, offering a more transparent and responsible approach.
