# automatisch

> **TL;DR:** Open-source Zapier alternative for self-hosted workflow automation connecting services like Twitter and Slack.

**`automatisch/automatisch`** · ⭐ 13,933 · 🔧 JavaScript

## What is it?
Automatisch is a self-hosted business workflow automation tool that enables users to connect different services (such as Twitter, Slack, and more) to automate their business processes. It positions itself as an open-source, free alternative to commercial solutions like Zapier and Integromat, requiring no programming knowledge to use.

## How does it work?
Automatisch operates as a self-hosted platform where users define workflows by connecting various services through visual or low-code interfaces. The system runs on the user's own servers (not in a proprietary cloud), allowing complete control over data storage and privacy compliance. Workflows are triggered when specific conditions are met across connected services, executing predefined actions automatically.

## Why is it important? (Core Value)
This project directly serves your objectives by providing a self-hosted alternative to SaaS automation tools like Zapier—exactly what you seek for homelab infrastructure and avoiding vendor lock-in. As a researcher focused on automation and workflow orchestration, Automatisch offers a practical, open-source platform you can host yourself while exploring how workflow engines are architected. It also aligns with your interest in self-hosted software, allowing you to study its internals as part of your knowledge base without the data privacy concerns inherent in cloud-based solutions.

## Key Features & Technologies
- Self-hosted deployment on user-controlled servers
- Connects services like Twitter, Slack, and more via webhooks/APIs
- No programming knowledge required to build workflows
- GDPR-compliant for European businesses
- Open-source with community-driven development
- No vendor lock-in — full data ownership

## Difference from Others
Unlike Zapier or Integromat, which are cloud-based SaaS products that store your data on their servers and charge per workflow/run, Automatisch is self-hosted and free. This means no monthly fees, no data privacy risks with sensitive information (healthcare/finance), and complete ownership of your workflows and data. However, it requires you to manage the infrastructure yourself rather than relying on a managed service.
